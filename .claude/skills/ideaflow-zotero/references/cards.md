# Card formats

## Idea Card

- **Research Question** — the testable question in one sentence.
- **Why It Matters** — the motivating observation and what would change if the hypothesis held.
- **Design** — proposed manipulations, controls and primary outcome.
- **Evidence and Open Questions** — what the literature supports, what is the researcher's hypothesis and what would distinguish competing explanations.
- **Next Decision** — a concrete design choice or search target.

When updating an existing idea, preserve earlier decisions that are still relevant. Keep the card attached to its collection as a standalone Zotero note.

## Memory Card

| Section | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| **Takeaway** | The finding or argument most relevant to the reading purpose |
| **Background** | The question the authors ask |
| **Method** | The study design, comparison and sample or tasks when verified |
| **Conclusion** | The authors' result and the conditions under which it holds |
| **Gap** | What the work leaves open and how it might inform this idea |

Record the evidence level, such as abstract, full text or supplied excerpts, and include the original source link. For full-text work, cite a section, figure or page when it helps the reader verify the note. A perspective piece or news article may have no experiment. Say so and summarize its argument or reporting basis.

Add **Reading Guide** for a closer read. Give a short summary, explain how the introduction frames the problem, and point to the first sections or figures to read. Label any short quotation and its source. Link to the original abstract and introduction for full passages.

For bilingual cards, put all English sections first, then the corresponding Simplified Chinese sections. Translate technical terms consistently and preserve numbers, qualifiers and links. Keep titles, collection names and tags in English unless the user requests another convention. Omit review questions unless requested.
